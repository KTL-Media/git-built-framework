# Common Unity Initiative

Part of the **F.A.C.T.S. Initiative** (Family • Action • Community • Transformation • Success).

> Together we build. Together we thrive. Together we rise.
> Building a self-reliant future — community, sustainability, legacy.

## About

The Common Unity Initiative is a collective of like-minded individuals and families building intentional, self-reliant communities. Preparation, not fear — sustainable systems, strong relationships, shared knowledge.

**Core values:** Unity • Sustainability • Empowerment • Integrity • Legacy
**Pillars:** Community • Sustainability • Self-Reliance • Empowerment • Legacy
**Rhythm:** Plan together • Build together • Grow together • Learn together • Thrive together

**Top 10 requirements for a successful off-grid community (2026):** reliable water supply, food production, sustainable energy, shelter & infrastructure, community security, waste management, health & wellness, skills & education, communication systems, strong governance.

**Top 10 agri-tech for off-grid living:** solar-powered irrigation, hydroponics & aquaponics, smart greenhouses, soil health monitoring, solar dryers & food preservers, composting & biodigesters, automated chicken coops, vertical farming towers, drone & AI crop monitoring, seed saving & genetic preservation.
