# Overview

This document lays out a teaching framework ("my students...") that reinterprets
Biblical Hebrew genealogical lists as intentional mistranslations concealing
African/Kushite-Sumerian-Akkadian stellar ritual and sorcery instructions. It
pairs a close phonetic word-study method with a full glossary of Mesopotamian
deities and a standard Golden Dawn–style Qabalistic Tree of Life reference.

**Core thesis:** English translations of Biblical Hebrew were shaped by
"Eurocentric" translators to replace spiritual/ritual function-language with
fabricated personal and family names — erasing an underlying African
cosmological system and serving a colonial/enslaving narrative purpose.

# 1. Method: Phonetic Fingerprinting

The central technique applied throughout: take a Hebrew verse conventionally
read as a genealogy, and instead render each word via its Akkadian/Sumerian
root and ritual/functional meaning rather than its conventional English gloss.

## Case Study: 1 Chronicles 8:12

> ובני אלפעל עבר ומשעם ושמד הוא בנה את־אונו ואת־לד ובנתיה

Conventional (KJV-style) translation: *"The sons of Elpaal; Eber, and Misham,
and Shamed, who built Ono, and Lod, with the towns thereof."*

Reframed, word by word:

| Hebrew | Transliteration | Conventional reading | Proposed root | Reframed meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ובני | ū-ḇə-nê | "sons of" | — | build / making |
| אלפעל | ’el-pa-‘al | "Elpaal" (name) | Akkadian *eleppu*, Sumerian GIŠ.MÁ | boat / vessel |
| עבר | ‘ê-ḇer | "Eber" (name) | Akkadian *ebēru*, Sumerian *BALA / *KU.RA | crossing over (celestial river/water) |
| ומשעם | ū-miš-‘ām | "Misham" (name) | Hebrew שעה *sha'ah* | to gaze / behold (stellar) |
| ושמד | wā-šā-meḏ | "Shamed" (name) | Akkadian *šamû*, Sumerian AN; cf. Arabic *samā’*, Hebrew *šāmayim* | sky / heavens (vastness) |
| הוא | hū | "who" | — | animating/functional particle |
| בנה | bā-nāh | "built" | Sumerian DÙ | build / bring forth (from ambition/psychology) |
| את־אונו | ’eṯ-’ō-w-nōw | "Ono" (place) | Akkadian *uṭṭatu*/Sumerian ŠEŠ ("truth," grain-measure); cf. Anu | truth from inner organs (liver divination) |
| ואת־לד | wə-’eṯ-lōḏ | "Lod" (place) | Akkadian *Laudo* | truth from bowing |
| ובנתיה | ū-ḇə-nō-ṯe-hā | "with the towns thereof" | — | constellations / celestial "daughters"/house |

**Interpretive claim:** Eber (עבר) is not a person or descendant of Elpaal —
it is a celestial/operational reference to "crossing over," and the verse as
a whole encodes a stellar ritual sequence (boat-building → crossing →
gazing at the heavens → oath/truth-taking → constellation observance) rather
than a genealogical record.

**Note on Eber/Hebrew root:** ties *ebēru* to Sumerian *KU.RA, floated as a
possible early phonetic relative of Arabic *Qur'an* (marked as speculative
in the source).

# 2. Noah, Shem, Ham, Japheth as Cosmology (not genealogy)

**Central claim:** these are not people but a four-part divination/cosmology
system layered onto a "linear descent" teaching model.

| Figure | Proposed cosmological referent | Key etymological claims |
|---|---|---|
| **Noah** (נח, phonetically NaCh) | Galaxy / serpentine motion of galactic chaos | From root shared with *Nachash* (serpent, gematria 358); linked to Kemetic serpent-enemy of Ra ("Nak"); name gematria 58 = sum of first 7 primes; those primes squared sum to 666, read as symbol of light's descent into physical form; also linked to Sumerian/Babylonian Anunnaki and to Enoch/Enmeduranki |
| **Shem** | Heavens / existing bodies of density | From *ShemMeSh* (sun) — heat disturbing the medium, producing sound; Shin (fire) as archetype; sun as mechanism transferring/lensing celestial "waters" |
| **Ham** (חם, KAM/KeM) | Dark matter / black light / connectivity | Reframed as a "quantum connectivity" numerological processing function, not a younger son; equator/black skin linked to proximity to solar magnetic field as "prototype of intelligence" |
| **Japheth** (יפת) | Earth / physical existence, material awareness ("the Ego Throne") | Root linked to *YoPeH* (beautiful, to shine) and to English "open"; three-letter breakdown (Hand/Mouth/Window); Kemetic *PeHt* ("to arrive at/obtain"); tied to Masonic Joppa lore; gematria proximity to "Makkah" (496, 3rd perfect number) |

**Four Kabbalistic Worlds mapping (explicit in source):**
Noah = Galaxy · Shem = Heavens · Ham = Dark Matter/Black Light · Japheth = Earth
