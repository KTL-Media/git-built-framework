## The claim

A 21-year-old woman claims she's making **$29,000/month** from AI-generated kids cartoons — with no camera, no hand-drawn animation, and no recorded voiceovers. Just an AI-powered production workflow.

## How it works

1. **Research** kids' YouTube channels to understand what gets attention.
2. **Study** the pacing, characters, hooks, and colors of successful videos.
3. **Create** an original toddler character from scratch.
4. **Generate** with an AI video generator (the author names **Korpi AI**) to bring the character to life.
5. **Produce** animated scenes and turn them into short episodes.
6. **Publish and track** what resonates with viewers.

## The loop

**research → create → generate → publish → improve → repeat**

No filming yourself. No animating every frame. No recording every line of dialogue. AI handles much of the production.

## The bigger play

The opportunity isn't copying someone else's characters or scripts — it's building an **original format** viewers want to come back to. Not one viral cartoon, but a **repeatable content system** around an original idea.

## The caveat (author's own)

Views don't guarantee revenue, and **YouTube's monetization rules still apply to AI-generated kids content**. (YouTube has tightened rules around AI-generated and "made for kids" content — check current policy before building on this.)

## Tool named

- **Korpi AI** — AI video generator for bringing characters and scenes to life.
