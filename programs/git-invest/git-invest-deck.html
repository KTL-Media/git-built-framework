Risks · Mitigations 08
What could break

Four risks, named up front

An AI that touches money has to earn trust faster than it earns attention.

  • The AI invents a grant, a deadline or a figure
  • We drift into investment advice or securities solicitation
  • Founders' financial data leaks or gets misused
  • Human review becomes the bottleneck as we grow
How we hedge

Guardrails built into the MVP

Every risk has an owner and a control before a single founder logs in.

  • Verified listings only; every match links to its source
  • We prepare founders — we don't broker deals; counsel reviews claims
  • Consent-first data: encrypted, founder-owned, shared only on opt-in
  • A rotating reviewer bench, with AI triage flagging what needs a human
GIT INVEST · MVP Launch 08 / 10
~1:30 — Read across, row by row: risk → hedge. Say plainly: "We are a preparation tool, not a broker or an adviser." The last row is the one people forget — if every output needs a human, the humans burn out. Triage fixes that.
