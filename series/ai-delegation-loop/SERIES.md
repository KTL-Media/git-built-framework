## The kit

The starter-kit PDF walks the reader through the loop hands-on:

1. **Process interview prompt** — extract the steps of anything you want to delegate
2. **Toolbox structure** — gather the tools, prompts, and resources the process needs
3. **Proof checklist** — define what "done" and "working" look like before you hand it off
4. **Playbook guidance** — turn the loop into a reusable playbook
