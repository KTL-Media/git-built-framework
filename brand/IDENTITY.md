# GIT BUILT — Brand Identity

**Subsidiary of Imaginarium · BUILD IDEAS. BREAK LIMITS. MAKE IMPACT.**

## Visual style

**Maximalist graffiti-steampunk.** Every asset carries the same DNA:

- **Base:** near-black backgrounds — riveted metal plates, dark steel textures
- **Machinery:** brass gears, wrenches, pipes, and industrial hardware woven into the composition
- **Light:** a single glowing bulb or energy source — the idea made visible
- **Color:** paint splashes in **gold, electric blue, and purple** cutting across the metal
- **Energy:** dense, layered, high-impact — never minimal, never corporate-clean

## Voice

Direct. Builder's language. Short sentences. No fluff. The brand talks like someone in the workshop, not a marketing department.

- Tagline: **BUILD IDEAS. BREAK LIMITS. MAKE IMPACT.**
- CTA pattern: *"Comment PLAYBOOK and I'll send you the free starter kit."*

## Series format rules

1. **Carousel:** 10 slides, 1080×1350. Slide 1 is the hook, slides 2–9 deliver the concept, slide 10 is always the PLAYBOOK CTA.
2. **Clips:** one vertical clip per slide — same branding, built for Reels/TikTok/Shorts.
3. **Reel:** the full carousel animated into a single video.
4. **Lead magnet:** every series ships with a free starter-kit PDF that teaches the concept hands-on.
5. **Approval gate:** nothing posts, sends, or publishes without explicit sign-off on the exact content.

## Usage

New series keep the same skeleton: `series/<series-slug>/` holds `carousel/`, `clips/`, the reel, and the starter-kit PDF. Build scripts live in `playbook-pattern/build/`.
